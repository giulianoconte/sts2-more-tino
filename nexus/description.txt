Rewrites every visible "tion" in the game to "tino" --
card names, potion names, relics, events, menus, run history, all of it.

- Corruption -> Corruptino
- Gigantification Potion -> Gigantificatino Potino
- Relic Collection -> Relic Collectino

English only. Doesn't affect gameplay.

My Mods
- SlayTheStats: https://www.nexusmods.com/slaythespire2/mods/349 - stats tracking
- ExitToDesktop: https://www.nexusmods.com/slaythespire2/mods/553 - qol
- RememberCardSort: https://www.nexusmods.com/slaythespire2/mods/801 - qol
- MoreTino: https://www.nexusmods.com/slaythespire2/mods/753 - memes

Requirements
- BaseLib v3.1.0 or later: https://www.nexusmods.com/slaythespire2/mods/103

Installation
Extract MoreTino-vX.X.X.zip into your mods folder so you end up with a
MoreTino/ folder containing MoreTino.dll, MoreTino.pck, and MoreTino.json.
